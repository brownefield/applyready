# ApplyReady — Product Doctrine

> ApplyReady helps you figure out what you're trying to reach, determines what it requires, shows what you're missing, and helps you get ready to move.

**Not affiliated with the U.S. federal government or any agency.**

---

## Positioning

ApplyReady is a **Readiness Workspace**, not a one-time diagnostic.

- A diagnostic happens once.
- A workspace stays with you until the outcome is reached.

Commercial frame:

> **“Tell me how ready I am” is interesting.**  
> **“Help me become ready for this thing I need” is valuable.**

The product is not the assessment. The product is:

> **ApplyReady turns an opportunity into a completion plan.**

Concretely:

> Show ApplyReady what you're trying to qualify for. We'll determine what it requires, what you already have, what's missing, and help you finish the rest.

## Universal object model

Everything in ApplyReady maps to five objects (owned conceptually by Waypoint; surfaced by ApplyReady):

```text
DESTINATION   What are you trying to reach?
REQUIREMENT   What must be true to get there?
EVIDENCE      What proves that it is true?
GAP           What's missing or insufficient?
ACTION        What makes the gap disappear?
```

### Module mapping

| Module | Role |
|--------|------|
| Mission Finder | Destination isn't known yet — find it |
| VentureFit | Multiple destinations — which fits |
| Mission Ready | Destination chosen — can I pursue it |
| Opportunity Ready | Specific opportunity — do I satisfy requirements |
| Federal Resume / ReResume | Specific job — does experience prove qualifications |
| Business vendor readiness | Specific network/contract — can the business prove eligibility |

Mission Finder and VentureFit are **modules inside ApplyReady**, not separate applications.

## Bring Your Own Opportunity

Primary interaction:

> **What are you trying to become ready for?**

Inputs:

- Paste a link
- Upload a document
- Describe it in plain language
- Choose an ApplyReady template

Output:

```text
Requirements → Evidence → Gaps → Actions → Generated artifacts → Submission readiness → Tracking
```

Questions asked should be **only those necessary to determine the destination's requirements** — not a static questionnaire library.

## Monetization

### FIND — free (acquisition)

- Mission Finder
- VentureFit
- General readiness diagnostic

Creates insight, trust, and an ApplyReady profile.

### READY — low-cost paid

Specific destination analysis.

- **Ready Check** (~$29) — detailed readiness analysis for one opportunity

### MOVE — primary revenue

- **Ready Packet** (~$99) — evidence mapping, gap plan, checklists, drafts, unresolved flags
- **Guided Ready** (~$249+) — human/operator review for high-stakes workflows

### Organizations (B2B)

Programs that need *other people* ready:

- Partner / operator plans (~$99–$299/mo starting point)
- Larger pricing by participants / cases

Existing Partner offer ($299 setup + $99/mo) remains the baseline B2B package until tested otherwise.

Pricing numbers are hypotheses to test — not locked.

## Government contracting posture

Do **not** sell “government contracting advice.”

SBA / APEX already provide free guidance.

Positioning:

> **APEX tells you what you need.**  
> **ApplyReady helps you organize, prove, prepare, and track it.**

## What we are not building right now

- Giant diagnostic library
- Marketplace
- Elaborate AI agent fleets
- Separate VentureFit app
- Separate Mission Finder backend
- Healthcare vertical
- Fancy dashboards
- Complicated subscription tier trees
- Dozens of readiness scores
- Many customer types at once

Risk to avoid: six clever products and zero indispensable ones.

---

## Recommended build order

1. Collapse Mission Finder + VentureFit into ApplyReady modules
2. Ship the universal object model in product surfaces
3. Build Bring Your Own Opportunity (PDF / link / text → requirements)
4. Build Ready Profile / Evidence Vault
5. Prove one paid workflow — **small-business opportunity / vendor readiness**
6. Charge before opening another vertical — get five people to pay for a Ready Packet, then automate what consumed time

---

## North-star sentence

> **Readiness by itself isn't the business. Closing the distance between “I want this” and “I'm ready for this” is the business.**
